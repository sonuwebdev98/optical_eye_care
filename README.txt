OPTICAL EYE CARE — Website starter

FILES
- index.html: complete responsive storefront website
- assets/diwali-poster.png: your supplied Diwali poster (if included)

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
3. For publishing, upload the folder contents to your hosting provider's public web root.

FEATURES
- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet and desktop
- Hero banner with Buy 1 Get 1 Free and Free Frame promotion
- Product catalogue with search and category filters
- Demo enquiry cart
- WhatsApp product enquiry and appointment enquiry
- Eye-care services, about, FAQ, contact section and map embed
- Call and WhatsApp links using the phone number from your poster

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE
- Product names, images, prices and categories are sample placeholders. Replace them with actual inventory.
- The poster's offer is displayed, but its eligibility, validity and terms should be confirmed by the shop and added clearly.
- The cart is an enquiry cart, not a payment checkout. No payment gateway, inventory database, customer account system or admin panel is connected.
- Appointment form sends a WhatsApp enquiry; it does not automatically confirm an appointment.
- Replace sample testimonial placeholders with genuine reviews.
- Confirm the Google Maps pin, opening hours, legal pages and privacy policy before publishing.
- The website is a static front-end starter. For live e-commerce, connect a secure backend, database, inventory, order management and payment gateway.
